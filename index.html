Athena

A personal, text-only assistant run by a single individual for their own use.

What it does

Athena is a private assistant built on the open-source Hermes Agent runtime. It runs on its owner's own computer and is used by one person: its owner. It is not a product, not a service, and has no other users.

Its main job is a morning brief — a short start-of-day summary assembled from the owner's own calendar, mail, and issue tracker, delivered to the owner's private messaging channel.

Google account access

Athena connects to its owner's own Google account through Google OAuth to read:

  • Google Calendar — today's events, to report the day's schedule
  • Gmail — recent messages, to flag anything that changes the day's priorities

It reads this data on the owner's behalf, on the owner's machine. See the privacy policy for how that data is handled.

Source
